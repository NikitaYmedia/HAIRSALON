Frequently asked questions

Can’t find your answer? Send us a message and we will reply within one working day.

Contact us
How long does a colour appointment take?

A root touch-up takes about 90 minutes, a full colour around two hours and balayage up to three hours. Your stylist will give you an exact estimate during the consultation.

Do you offer a consultation before colouring?

Yes — every colour service begins with a free consultation. We talk through your goals, check your hair history and, if needed, do a strand test.

What products do you use?

We work with professional, salon-grade brands that are kind to your hair. Ask us about vegan and ammonia-free options.

How do I cancel or reschedule?

Please let us know at least 24 hours in advance by phone or email, and we will happily find you a new time.

Do prices depend on hair length?

All prices are starting prices. Very long or thick hair may need extra time and product, which we will always discuss with you before we begin.
